Federated Traffic-Flow Prediction (PS-003)

Demo: saved historical traffic replayed through models trained with Federated Averaging (FedAvg)
Please read first
  • The "intersections" are simulated: four slices of one sensor's data (I-94, Minnesota, USA, 2012-2018), not real separate intersections.
  • This page replays saved historical data from the test period (5 Oct 2017 - 30 Sep 2018). There is no live feed and nothing is trained here.
  • Federated learning keeps raw data on the clients but is not full privacy: model updates can leak information. Secure aggregation and differential privacy are future work.

Try it: forecast one hour ahead
